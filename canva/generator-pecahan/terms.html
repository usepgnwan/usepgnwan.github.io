Welcome to Generator Pecahan, an app for Canva that helps students and teachers create accurate visuals of common fractions for learning materials. By installing or using this app, you agree to these Terms and Conditions.

1Tentang AplikasiAbout the App

Aplikasi ini memungkinkan Anda memilih bentuk geometri (Lingkaran, Persegi, Persegi Panjang, Segitiga, Oval, Heksagon, Batang, Pentagon, Oktagon, Belah Ketupat, dan Donat/Cincin), memasukkan pembilang dan penyebut beserta bagian yang diarsir, mengatur warna latar, warna arsiran, warna bagian tanpa arsiran, serta warna garis dan teks, memilih font dari koleksi Canva, lalu menambahkan visual pecahan ke desain Canva. Visual yang sudah ada di desain dapat diperbarui kapan saja.

The app lets you choose a geometric shape (Circle, Square, Rectangle, Triangle, Oval, Hexagon, Bar, Pentagon, Octagon, Rhombus, and Donut/Ring), enter the numerator and denominator along with the shaded parts, set the background, shaded, unshaded, and outline/text colors, choose a font from Canva's collection, then add the fraction visual to your Canva design. Visuals already in a design can be updated at any time.
